Patching Memories

Narratives of Displacement Across South Africa

Find Out More

Project Description

This project seeks to complement the District Six Museum’s physical space with an interactive digital space. Rather than presenting forced displacement only as a history experienced in one locality, the digital platform will allow users to explore its broader scale while recognizing the individual experiences that make up that history. Digital space provides the freedom to travel across geographical distances in an accessible way, connecting individual stories from different communities and regions. By mapping these experiences, the project will place the history of District Six within a broader history of forced displacement in South Africa while maintaining attention to individual experiences.The project will also create an interactive space for people to contribute to the narrative of forced displacement. It provides an opportunity for communities to contribute their own narratives, including experiences that may not currently be represented in the museum.

Audience

People who have experienced forced displacement in South Africa and their descendants. The project will provide a space where their experiences can be represented, explored, and connected to other narratives of displacement across the country. Also we target people who want to learn what it feels like to experience forced displacement and better understand its broader historical and social impact through personal narratives.
